Find your kind of people.

A useful audience is more than an age range. Start with where customers are, what they care about, and what they need from you.

📍Local reach

Your neighborhood regulars

People near your shop or service area who could become repeat customers. A good starting point for cafes, stores and in-person services.

🛍️Ready to shop

People looking for a solution

Potential customers already searching for a product or service like yours. Helpful when intent matters more than broad awareness.

Try: search ads, product listings↗ Intent
✨Shared interests

Your future fan community

People who follow topics, hobbies and creators related to your brand. Great for visual stories, launches and building recognition.

Try: short video, social posts♡ Interests
🏡Households

Families and local groups

Parents, caregivers and community groups choosing places and services for more than one person. Think useful offers and trusted recommendations.

Try: local groups, email⌂ Community
📬Come back again

Customers you already know

Past customers and people who opted in to hear from you. Follow-up messages and helpful updates can encourage a second visit.

Try: email, loyalty updates↻ Retention

Shoppers beyond your town

If you can sell and ship online, connect with people who share a need or style — wherever they happen to be.

Pick the right place to show up

A channel for every kind of story.

Each advertising medium does something different. Compare the basics, then test one or two that fit your customers and budget.

🔎Intent

Search advertising

Show up when someone is actively looking for a nearby service or product. Strong for capturing existing demand.

Best for: visits, leadsBudget: flexible
📱Discovery

Social media ads

Reach people by location and interests with photos, short video and offers. Useful for building awareness and community.

Best for: awareness, salesBudget: flexible
🎬Storytelling

Short-form video

Show how your product works or what makes your place special. Creative, authentic clips can help people discover you.

Best for: discoveryNeeds: video creative
🗺️Nearby

Local listings & maps

Make it easy for nearby customers to find your hours, location and reviews when they are deciding where to go.

Best for: store visitsStart: claim listing
✉️Loyalty

Email & newsletters

Keep in touch with customers who opted in. Share useful updates and offers without paying for every impression.

Best for: repeat visitsNeeds: opt-in list

Community partnerships

Work with local events, creators or complementary businesses to get an introduction from someone customers trust.

Best for: local reachNeeds: good fit
